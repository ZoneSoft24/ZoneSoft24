## 🧠 Who We Are

<img align="right" alt="Coding" width="380" src="https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/74038190/229223263-cf2e4b07-2615-4f87-9c38-e37600f8381a.gif"/>

> **ZoneSoft** is the official software division of **IT-Zone Club** at Ton Duc Thang University (TDTU), Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

We are a passionate collective of **software engineers**, **product designers**, and **tech enthusiasts** — united by one goal: building innovative, real-world solutions that matter.

Founded in **January 2024**, ZoneSoft bridges the gap between **academic knowledge** and **practical application**, transforming bold ideas into scalable, production-grade products.

- 🎯 **Mission** — Empower young tech talent to create meaningful, impactful software through collaboration and innovation
- 🔭 **Vision** — Become Vietnam's most respected student-led software team, known for real products and future-ready engineers
- 🌱 **Culture** — Learning by building. Growing by shipping. Winning together.
